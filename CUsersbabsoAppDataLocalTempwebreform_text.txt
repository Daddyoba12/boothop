Why I've changed my thinking
When we first discussed this, I assumed registration was a small hurdle. Your TikTok analytics changed that.
You have:
843 Page Views 
358 Landing Page Views 
Only 1 Complete Registration 
That tells me registration is acting as a wall, not a doorway.
At the same time, BootHop is a marketplace. If someone can complete too much without registering, you risk people browsing indefinitely or scraping information. So I'd use a hybrid approach:
Let users begin their task without friction. 
Stop them just before they receive something valuable (like seeing matching travellers or parcel opportunities). 
Then ask them to register to unlock the results. 
This is the same principle used by many successful marketplaces: users experience value first, then create an account to access it.
